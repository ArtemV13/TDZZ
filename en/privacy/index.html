Personal data processing policy

Version dated 21 September 2026. Applies to tdzz.ru and all its pages, including the English version. In case of discrepancy the Russian text prevails.

1. General

1.1. This Policy is drawn up in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 “On Personal Data” of the Russian Federation and sets out how personal data of users of the website tdzz.ru (the “Website”) and of other persons entering into civil-law relations with the Operator is processed and protected.

1.2. Data controller (the “Operator”): Zerno Zavolzhya Trading House Limited Liability Company (OOO “TD “Zerno Zavolzhya”), Tax ID (INN) 3445091590, OGRN 1073460005651. Registered address: 5 Petra Velikogo Sq., office 504, Lipetsk, 398008, Russia. Operating office: 1A Parkhomenko St., Volgograd, 400087, Russia. E-mail for personal data matters: info@tdzz.ru.

1.3. The Policy is intended to protect the rights and freedoms of individuals whose personal data is processed, including the right to privacy.

1.4. Using the Website, including submitting any form on it, constitutes acceptance of this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please refrain from using the Website and from providing your data to the Operator.

2. Definitions

Personal data — any information relating directly or indirectly to an identified or identifiable natural person (data subject).

Processing — any operation or set of operations performed on personal data, with or without automated means: collection, recording, organisation, accumulation, storage, updating, retrieval, use, transfer (provision, access), anonymisation, blocking, erasure, destruction.

User — any person accessing and using the Website via the Internet.

Cookie — a small piece of data that a website sends to the user’s browser and the browser returns with subsequent requests.

3. Data we process

3.1. Data provided by the user when completing forms on the Website (“Sell grain”, “Request an offer”, “Contact us” and others): full name; company name; position; phone number, including messenger numbers; e-mail address; region, district or country; details of the lot (crop, quality, volume, delivery point, basis, destination) and any other information the user voluntarily enters in the form fields or comments.

3.2. Data collected automatically when the Website is visited: IP address, date and time of the visit, browser and operating system data, referring page, actions on the Website. This information is collected through web-server logs and web analytics services (Yandex.Metrica) in anonymised form and is used to keep the Website running, to prevent spam and to analyse traffic.

3.3. Counterparty onboarding documents (KYC questionnaire, constitutional and other documents) are not submitted through Website forms but within a separate onboarding procedure via a channel agreed with the Operator.

3.4. The Operator does not process special categories of personal data (race, ethnicity, political opinions, religious beliefs, health, etc.) or biometric data. The Operator does not verify the accuracy of data provided by users and assumes that users provide accurate information about themselves.

4. Purposes of processing

4.1. Personal data is processed for the following purposes:

  • receiving, reviewing and responding to enquiries, applications and commercial requests submitted through the Website;
  • routing the request to the relevant department of the Operator (procurement, export, logistics, corporate matters) and conducting business correspondence;
  • preparing commercial offers, agreeing terms of purchase, supply and storage of agricultural products, concluding and performing contracts;
  • performing counterparty due diligence (KYC) at the counterparty’s request;
  • ensuring the operation and security of the Website, protection against spam and unauthorised access;
  • analysing traffic and improving the Website on the basis of anonymised data;
  • fulfilling obligations imposed on the Operator by the laws of the Russian Federation.

4.2. The Operator does not use user data for mass marketing mailings without separate consent and does not transfer it to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Legal basis and consent

5.1. Processing is based on: the consent of the data subject expressed when submitting a form on the Website (the “I consent to the processing of my personal data” checkbox); the necessity to conclude and perform a contract to which the data subject or the organisation he or she represents is a party; the legitimate interests of the Operator that do not infringe the rights of the data subject; the requirements of Russian law.

5.2. Consent is given freely, of the person’s own will and in his or her own interest, and is specific, informed and conscious. The user may withdraw consent at any time by sending a notice to info@tdzz.ru marked “Withdrawal of consent to personal data processing”. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it was received and does not prevent processing for which other legal grounds exist.

6. Processing principles

  • processing is carried out lawfully and fairly;
  • processing is limited to specific, pre-defined and legitimate purposes;
  • only data that meets the purposes of processing is processed; excessive data is not collected;
  • accuracy, sufficiency and relevance of data are ensured;
  • data is stored no longer than the purposes of processing require, unless another period is set by law or contract;
  • databases containing personal data processed for incompatible purposes are not merged.

7. How data is processed

7.1. The Operator processes personal data with and without automated means. Data submitted through Website forms is sent to the Operator’s corporate e-mail, may be recorded in the enquiry log on the Website server and entered into the Operator’s CRM and other internal information systems.

7.2. Access to personal data is granted only to authorised employees of the Operator who need it to perform their duties and who are bound by confidentiality obligations.

7.3. The Operator may entrust processing to third parties under contract (hosting provider, e-mail, notification and web analytics service providers) provided they maintain confidentiality and comply with data protection requirements. Personal data is not sold, exchanged or published.

7.4. Personal data is disclosed to state authorities only in the cases and in the manner provided for by the laws of the Russian Federation.

7.5. Cross-border transfer of personal data is not a purpose of the Operator. Should the services referred to in clause 7.3 require transfer of data to a foreign state, the Operator complies with Article 12 of Law No. 152-FZ.

8. Retention

8.1. Personal data is stored in a form that allows the data subject to be identified for no longer than the purposes of processing require: enquiries that did not result in a contract — up to 3 years from the last interaction; data related to concluded contracts — for the term of the contract and the retention periods set by accounting and archival legislation.

8.2. Once the purposes of processing are achieved, upon withdrawal of consent (where no other legal grounds exist) or upon expiry of retention periods, personal data is destroyed or anonymised.

9. Rights of the data subject

9.1. The data subject has the right to:

  • obtain information on whether, why, how and for how long his or her personal data is processed, to whom it may be transferred, and other information provided for by Article 14 of Law No. 152-FZ;
  • request that personal data be updated, blocked or destroyed if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or not necessary for the stated purpose of processing;
  • withdraw consent to processing;
  • appeal against the Operator’s actions or omissions to the authorised body for the protection of data subjects’ rights (Roskomnadzor) or in court;
  • exercise other rights provided for by the laws of the Russian Federation.

9.2. To exercise these rights, the data subject sends a request to info@tdzz.ru or to the Operator’s postal address. The request must contain information allowing the data subject and his or her data to be identified (full name and the contact details given when contacting the Operator through the Website). The Operator reviews the request and responds within the time limits set by Law No. 152-FZ (as a rule, no later than 10 business days).

10. Data protection

10.1. The Operator takes the necessary legal, organisational and technical measures to protect personal data from unlawful or accidental access, destruction, alteration, blocking, copying, provision, dissemination and other unlawful actions, including:

  • appointing a person responsible for organising personal data processing;
  • adopting internal regulations on personal data processing and protection and familiarising employees with them;
  • restricting and recording access to data and differentiating user rights in information systems;
  • using an encrypted connection (HTTPS) for data submitted from the Website, anti-virus protection and protection against spam and automated requests;
  • backing up data and monitoring its integrity;
  • internal control of compliance of processing with legal requirements.

11. Cookies and analytics

11.1. The Website uses cookies and web analytics services to operate correctly, remember the selected language and analyse traffic in anonymised form. Users can disable cookies in their browser settings; some Website functions may then not work correctly.

11.2. The Website may contain embedded elements of third-party services (for example, the OpenStreetMap office map). When they load, the user’s browser connects to the servers of the respective service, whose data processing is governed by its own policy.

12. Third-party websites

The Website may contain links to third-party websites. The Operator does not control such websites and is not responsible for their content or their personal data practices.

13. Changes to the Policy

The Operator may amend this Policy. A new version takes effect once published on the Website, unless the version itself provides otherwise. The current version is always available at tdzz.ru/en/privacy.html.
